- Provide a kicker, one display title, one subtitle, and a byline of three lines: name, role, date. On the closing slide, provide the kicker, the title and a byline of two lines: name, contact.

- Keep the three zones: kicker at the top margin, title and subtitle centred in the space between, byline at the bottom margin.
- The byline sits under a hairline with the 64px accent mark. Do not add other rules or shapes.
- The opening slide carries no slide number; the closing variant does. For the closing variant, add `data-variant="Closing"` to the section.
