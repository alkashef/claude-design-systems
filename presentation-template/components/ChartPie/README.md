Pie chart with up to five slices, a legend list with shares beside it, and shares printed inside large slices.

- Edit `labels` and `values` in `data-chart`. Order slices largest first and fold small ones into Other.
- Use a pie only to show how one whole splits, at a glance. To compare close values, use the bar chart.
- Slices take chart colours 1 to 4 in order. Do not use more than five slices.
- Write the source line under the chart.
